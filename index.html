About Me

My current focus is frontend development with React and TypeScript. I also have introductory experience with backend development, including writing simple APIs, testing requests with Postman, and writing basic SQL queries. I'm continuing to develop my understanding of databases, cloud computing, Linux, and software engineering fundamentals.

I enjoy understanding how the different parts of an application work together — from the user interface to backend services and data storage. My goal is to build useful, reliable applications while becoming more confident in problem-solving, testing, debugging, and making technical decisions.

I'm here to learn, build, improve, and keep moving forward.

If you're a software engineer, mentor, or fellow learner, I'd love to connect, exchange ideas, and learn from your experience.
